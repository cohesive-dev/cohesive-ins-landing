## Sources and claim boundaries

Official sources were researched September 14, 2026. Links appear beside the relevant facts.
Some regulator hosts reject direct automated fetches (Arizona ROC, NJ Consumer Affairs and
Mass.gov); current official-domain search extracts were available. Arizona's handyman
exemption was also checked against the legislature's full statute. Sources are not evidence
of a particular insurer's appetite or a blanket guarantee of coverage.

- Arizona ROC, pool construction B-5 vs minor service R-6 and excluded backyard structures:
  https://roc.az.gov/license-classifications
- Arizona legislature, section 32-1121(A)(14), (D): minor-work exemption, aggregate price,
  permit/larger-project limitations, advertising disclosure and specified task exclusions:
  https://www.azleg.gov/ars/32/01121.htm
  The statute's specific counting rules qualify the threshold; do not simplify it into
  permission for every invoice below $1,000 or state a $1,000-inclusive threshold.
- Florida DBPR: pool specialties and prohibition on direct public contracts for the cited
  specialty license categories. Do not extend that rule to full pool contractors:
  https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/construction-industry/faqs/
- Connecticut DEEP: licensed arboriculture includes pruning/trimming and tree-health work;
  pesticide scope and business registration. No blanket assertion that every removal-only
  business requires that particular credential:
  https://portal.ct.gov/deep/pesticides/arborist/commercial-arborist-license
- Louisiana LDAF: arborist vs utility-arborist scope, insurance evidence and written contracts:
  https://www.ldaf.la.gov/land/arborists
- Massachusetts HIC vs CSL, project scope and ordinary repair examples:
  https://www.mass.gov/info-details/hic-contractor-resources
- Massachusetts lead-safe renovation (not all deleading authorization, not policy coverage):
  https://www.mass.gov/info-details/lead-safe-renovation-for-contractors
- Pennsylvania registration evidence: $50k personal injury and $50k property damage,
  not workmanship protection or recommended limits for every contract:
  https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/businesses-and-organizations/home-improvement-contractor-registration/frequently-asked-questions/
- NYC DCWP checklist: local scope and explicit rejection of ACORD as proof of NY workers'
  compensation. This is not a blanket rejection of ACORD for GL or a statewide license rule:
  https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/businesses/license-checklist-home-improvement-contractor.page
- NJ home improvement business: $500k per-occurrence GL, WC unless exempt and additional security:
  https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/hic/Pages/FAQ.aspx
- Oklahoma CIB: current $500k residential / $1m commercial roofing evidence and separate
  FUTURE residential endorsement transition (2027 transition, 2028 requirement):
  https://oklahoma.gov/cib/your-industry/roofing/active-roofing-contractor-requirements.html
  https://oklahoma.gov/cib/news/new-residential-roofing-endorsement-required-by-house-bill-1628.html
- Texas TDI: designated-area building eligibility for TWIA and inspection timing, not a
  statewide GL rule or insurance supplied by a WPI-8:
  https://tdi.texas.gov/WIND/generalquestio.html
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/inspectionproc.html
