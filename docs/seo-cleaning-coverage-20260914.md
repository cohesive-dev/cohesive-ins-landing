## Claim boundaries and sources checked September 14

The internal cleaning playbook records comparisons where the property-damage gap matters.
It does not establish that most carriers exclude all property damage, or verify a current
carrier-wide exclusion. No named client, private premium or unverified denial is published.

- https://www.thimble.com/industry/cleaning-business-insurance/house-cleaner
  describes the common CCC gap and selected-limit protection for property worked on.
- https://www.nextinsurance.com/glossary/care-custody-and-control/
  explains CCC and that an applicable exclusion can prevent recovery.
- https://www.thimble.com/industry/cleaning-business-insurance/pressure-washer
  describes customer-property protection and property being worked on. House cleaning and
  pressure washing are kept distinct; no universal mandatory add-on is asserted.
- https://www.tdi.texas.gov/pubs/pc/pcgenliab.html
  supports comparing full terms and exclusions, not just price.
- https://www.tdi.texas.gov/certificates/faq.html
  supports the limits of certificates. No Texas requirement is generalized nationally.
- https://www.thimble.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Thimble_Appetite_Guide_Apr02_2026.pdf
  lists a specific below-30-feet tree trimming/pruning activity. This is labelled appetite
  evidence, not a universal cutoff or a quoted policy exclusion. Tree guide asks for actual
  height wording and its measurement, removal, cranes and utility restrictions.

Pool installation/gunite/shotcrete, excavation, subsidence, utilities and subcontracting
are coverage-review questions; no carrier knockout, coverage promise or new price is claimed.
The examples in the cleaning guide are hypothetical, not paid-claim reports. Marketing
pages do not replace the issued form. No placement eligibility was expanded.
